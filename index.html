Why train with us

Built for beginners, serious about getting better

No experience needed

No tryouts, no cuts. Most of our members started from zero. Every practice includes instruction geared to whoever shows up.

A real community

Practices, competitions, open mats, team dinners, and a WhatsApp community that's always awake. You'll know everyone by week two.

Compete if you want to

We travel to New England tournaments in every discipline we offer. Competing is always optional; supporting your teammates from the sidelines counts too.
