Step 1

Join the WhatsApp

All practice updates, cancellations, carpools, and open mats go through our WhatsApp community. It's also where you can ask anything before you show up.

Open the invite link →
Step 2

Come to a practice

Pick any session on the schedule, wear athletic clothes, bring water and your HUID. An officer will meet you and get you started.

Step 3

Become a member

Once you've decided it's for you, we'll walk you through the Club Sports registration and collect dues. Then you're in: every branch, all semester.

Cost

Dues & financial aid

Membership dues start at $20 and increase only if you'd like a uniform (a gi or singlet) or club merchandise. Dues cover every discipline the club offers.

Financial aid is available for undergraduates. Cost should never be the reason you don't train. Email us or talk to any officer; it's quick and confidential.

Ask about financial aid

What dues pay for

  • Coaching and instruction
  • Mats, training gear, and first-aid supplies
  • Tournament registration and travel subsidies
  • Club socials and events
Support the club

Donate

HCSC is student-run and funded largely by dues. Gifts from alumni, families, and friends go directly toward coaching, competition travel, and financial aid so that any Harvard student can train.

To make a gift, please email the club and we'll send you current instructions for giving through Harvard. Thank you for backing student combat sports.

Other ways to help

  • Alumni & coaches: come run a clinic or open mat.
  • Gear: gently used gis, singlets, and wrestling shoes find new owners fast.
  • Word of mouth: send a friend who's been meaning to try.
