Disciplines

Each discipline gets a nav link, a card on the home page, a color, and its own page. Untick Active to hide one without deleting it.

Adding a new sport: fill in a new entry, click Create page next to it (this copies the template into a new page), save, then replace the [BRACKETED] text on the new page on github.com when you're ready.

Photos

Pick photos and they upload straight to the website; big phone photos are shrunk automatically. The public gallery refreshes about a minute after upload. Only upload photos of people who are fine appearing on a public site.

Add to the gallery
